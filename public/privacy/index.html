Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

GECK REKT uses this website to share information about the game and collect optional mailing-list subscriptions.

Analytics

If you accept analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand general website usage, such as visits, approximate location, device type, page views, QR visits, and clicks on social links. Google Analytics may use cookies and similar identifiers. We do not send email addresses or other form contents to Google Analytics.

If you reject analytics, Google Analytics is not loaded and these optional analytics events are not sent. The site, social links, and mailing-list form continue to work.

Your choice

You can change your analytics choice at any time.

Mailing list

If you subscribe, your email and selected interests are processed by our email service provider to send the updates you request. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in an email.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the GECK REKT team through the website’s social channels.
