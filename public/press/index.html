The short version

Guns. Tongues.
Glory.

Gecks with guns! BLAST your friends in 2–4 player online and local battles. LICK them to steal weapons or fling them across the map. VOTE on rules that stack into increasingly chaotic combinations. Adapt fast or get REKT.

Geck REKT is a competitive party arena shooter starring Gecks, little creatures somewhere between a gecko and a frog.

  • Fast rounds. One-hit kills. Battle your friends with guns and long, grabby tongues.
  • Steal, throw, and improvise. Grab a weapon, fling an opponent across the arena, or turn a desperate scramble for a gun into a last-second victory.
  • Vote on new rules. Every few rounds, call down meteor showers, make corpses explode, or change how weapons and tongues behave.
  • Adapt to stacking chaos. Each rule stays active for the rest of the match. Make your friends regret their vote.
  • Bring 2–4 players. Play locally or online.

Inspired by games like Stick Fight: The Game and Duck Game, Geck REKT combines quick rounds and physical comedy.
